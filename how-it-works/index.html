The alternatives

Why not just...

Use the AI already in our tools?

Each assistant only sees its own tool. It cannot answer a question that crosses teams, and most of your questions do.

Wire it up ourselves?

ChatGPT and an automation tool work until the person who built it gets busy or leaves. Nobody owns it, and nobody else knows how it works.

Hire an operations person?

A good hire, but one person cannot build and run systems across every team. What they know leaves when they do.

Bring in a consultant?

Many hand you insights and a plan, then leave. We build the system that acts on them, train your team, and it stays yours.
