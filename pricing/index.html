Questions

Pricing questions, answered.

Do we need a Blueprint to start?

Not for a first build. One piece is small enough to scope together in a conversation. A whole function or a full rollout starts with the Blueprint.

Why is the Blueprint paid?

Because it is real work, and it is yours. You leave with the map, the build list and a fixed quote whether or not we build any of it. See an example Blueprint.

Does the Blueprint fee come off the build?

Yes. Go ahead with a function build or a full rollout and the full $5,000 is credited toward it.

Why only four builds at a time?

Because every build gets a senior operator inside your company, and four is what we can do well at once. The Blueprint and the monthly plans are not capped.

Why do the builds say "from"?

Every company runs a different set of tools. You get a fixed price for your build, in writing, before any build work starts.

Do you bill by the hour?

No. Each stage has a fixed price, agreed before it starts.

Do we have to keep paying you after launch?

No. The system and its data are yours, and your team is trained to run it. The monthly plans are there if you would rather we did.

What do the AI models cost to run?

You hold your own accounts with the model providers and pay them directly, at cost. We do not mark usage up.
