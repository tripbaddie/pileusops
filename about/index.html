About Pileus Ops

You are running a company nobody designed.

It starts with a good decision. Sales needs a CRM, so sales buys one. Then marketing. Then finance. Then support.

Ten good decisions later, you have a dozen subscriptions that have never met, and your best people working as the wiring between them.

For twenty years, that was the deal. Rent software built for everyone, then pay people to make it fit you. Software built just for you was for companies a hundred times your size.

AI ended that deal. Software built around one company can now cost less than the subscriptions it replaces. And AI can do the work in between, once it can see the whole company.

So that is what we build. Custom agents and apps across every function, connecting the tools worth keeping and replacing the ones that are not. Designed by operators who have run those functions.

Most of it sits where your customers never look. That is where our name comes from.

The name

The part you see is the smallest part.

Pileus (PIE-lee-us) is the cap of a mushroom. It is the only part anyone notices.

The real organism is underneath. Mycelium, a web of fine threads that can run for miles, moving water and nutrients wherever the forest needs them. The cap is just where it surfaces.

Companies work the same way. Your customers see the cap: the product that works, the invoice that is right, the reply that came back within the hour. They never see the apps, the data and the handoffs that had to connect to make it happen.

That is the part we build. Get it right and growth stops feeling like strain.

The word has an older meaning too. In ancient Rome, the pileus was the cap given to a person on the day they were freed, worn in public as a sign they belonged to themselves.

We hold onto that meaning as well. What we build is yours, to run with us or without us.
