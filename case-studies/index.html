What stays with the CEO

The CEO approves the social posts and comments and sends most of the drafts. That is the editor's job for a team of five, not the five jobs.

Launch week

Extra agents ran for a product launch and took the workload closer to six roles. They were switched off when it ended.

What both show

A small team, doing what a small team skips.

Work that would not get done

A startup this size would hire three of these roles at most and go without the rest. Here the rest runs every day.

Pages you own, not seats you rent

Every report and tracker is a page built for that company. No price per seat, and nothing to migrate when a vendor changes its plans.

You stay the editor

Agents research, draft and prepare. People approve what matters. See how approval works.
