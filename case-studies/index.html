What one system makes possible

The whole picture

Yesterday, on one page, before 8am.

Payments, product usage and traffic, read together and compared with the day and the week before. Every part of the business gets a status: OK, watch or fix. It checks its own numbers too, so bot traffic never drives a decision.

Listening to learning

What travelers ask becomes what gets made.

The system follows travel conversations on forums and social groups every day and joins the ones where the company can help. The questions people keep asking become next month's content.

Draws onSocial listening · Search · The content plan

Competitors to content

It knows what the competition did this week, and answers it.

It tracks where rivals show up in search and in AI answers and how they market themselves, then turns what it found into a plan and hands the content agents their briefs.

Draws onCompetitors · Search and AI answers · Marketing

Marketing

A full content calendar, with no marketing team.

Every Friday the week ahead is planned, designed and scheduled across every channel, using what the competitor and listening agents found. The founder films a few videos and approves the rest.

Draws onCompetitor findings · Social listening · What performed

Outreach and press

The pitch is written. So is the reply.

Every day it finds and vets creators, publications and journalist requests, writes each pitch in the founder's voice, and drafts the answer when someone writes back. Every contact lives in one record, so nobody is pitched twice.

Draws onThe inbox · One contact record · The events calendar

New markets

A new market, in motion the week it was chosen.

When the founder picked a new audience to go after, nothing had to be bought or hired. The research, content, outreach and press for it were running within days.

Draws onResearch · Content · Outreach · Press

The system itself

It reviews its own work.

Each week it reads what every agent did, pulls out what worked and what did not, and proposes its own fixes. The founder answers yes or no.
