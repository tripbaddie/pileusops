01AI travel planning app · Startup

One founder, part-time. No employees.

A consumer app that plans trips. One founder runs its marketing, outreach, press and reporting through a system of agents, and approves what goes out.

What it does

The morning report

Yesterday, on one page, before 8am.

Payments, product usage and traffic, compared with the day before and the week before. Every part of the business gets a status: OK, watch or fix. It checks its own numbers too, so bot traffic never drives a decision.

Marketing

A full content calendar, with no marketing team.

Every Friday the agents plan, design and schedule the week ahead across every channel. The founder films a few videos and approves the rest.

Outreach

The pitch is written. So is the reply.

Every day the system finds and vets creators and publications, writes each pitch in the founder's voice, and drafts the answer when someone writes back.

Press

A press desk for a company of one.

It reads the day's journalist requests, answers the ones the founder can speak to, and pitches local outlets when the timing is right.

New markets

A new market, in motion the week it was chosen.

When the founder picked a new audience to go after, the research, content, outreach and press for it were running within days.

Chief of staff

A system that reviews itself.

Each week it reads what every agent did, ranks what mattered and proposes its own fixes. The founder answers yes or no.

In two months

More than 1,500 creators and publications vetted, 73 pages published for search, and three independent write-ups of the app.

02AI sales platform · Startup

One CEO. Every function outside engineering.

A B2B sales platform. One CEO runs its sales, marketing, press, support and reporting through a system of agents, and approves what goes out.

What it does

The morning report

It sees the problem before it is one.

Every part of the business on one page, compared day over day. It shows where things are breaking and what needs attention, calls out anything unusual, and warns of trouble before it starts, from the whole company down to a single account.

Conversations

Every reply, already written.

It reads the inbox, LinkedIn and everywhere else a conversation is happening, and tees up each response to approve. The background on the person sits beside the draft, ready to check on the spot.

Go to market

Sales, marketing and a press desk, run by one person.

Each runs on its own strategy, and each strategy tunes itself. The system watches what is working and adjusts as it goes.

CRM

A CRM we built, because renting one cost too much.

It does what this company needs and plugs into everything else. Every prospect and customer carries their whole history, from email to live chat to how they use the product.

Product

Where users get stuck, without watching a single recording.

AI reviews the session recordings and calls out the errors, and the exact points where new and existing users run into trouble.

Support

A customer writes in. The answer is already waiting.

The AI opens their account, works out what is going on, suggests what to improve and drafts the reply. The team steps in only when it wants to.
