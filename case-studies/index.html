01AI travel planning app · Startup

One founder, part-time. No employees.

A consumer app that plans trips. Its marketing, outreach, reporting and press run as agents on a schedule. The founder reviews what they produce and approves what goes out.

4

5

What we built

A marketing team of agents

Agents that plan, design and schedule the social content, run creator and press outreach, and keep the search content growing.

A morning report

One page that reads payments, product usage and traffic, and says what to do next.

A chief of staff

A weekly review of everything the agents did, ending in the decisions the founder needs to make.

What changed for the founder

Executive function

A chief of staff that never loses the thread.

A weekly review ranks what mattered and proposes the fix for each. Anything left open comes back the next week.

Reporting

Yesterday, on one page, before 8am.

Payments, product usage and traffic in one morning report. Nobody builds it.

Visibility

Numbers the founder can trust.

Every part of the business gets a status each morning: OK, watch or fix.

Time to decision

Decisions arrive ready to answer.

Each agent ends with a short list of what it needs: one action each, with a time estimate.

Time to result

A new market, in motion the week it was chosen.

When the founder picked a new audience to go after, the research, content, outreach and press for it were running within days.

In two months

More than 1,500 creators and publications vetted, 73 pages published for search, and three independent write-ups of the app.

02AI sales platform · Startup

One CEO. Every function outside engineering.

A B2B sales platform. Its own outbound, content, reporting and billing follow-ups run as agents. The CEO edits and approves.

5

6

What we built

An outbound engine

Agents that research and write the day's sales pitches, partner outreach and press answers, and track every reply.

A morning report

One page that reads nine systems, from billing to support to search, and says what to do next.

A back-office assistant

Agents that brief every meeting, follow up failed payments and draft a note to each new signup.

What changed for the CEO

Executive function

The day starts already briefed.

A brief for every meeting, and a report on what moved overnight and what to do about it.

Reporting

Nine systems, one page, every morning.

Billing, product usage, support, the CRM and search, read together. Nobody exports a spreadsheet.

Visibility

Every pipeline on one board.

Sales pitches, partner outreach and press each sit on one tracker, sorted from pending to won.

Time to decision

Approve, edit or skip.

Replies arrive sorted, each with a proposed answer. The CEO approves it or edits it.

Time to result

Nothing waits for someone to notice.

A failed payment gets a follow-up and a new signup gets a personal note, without anyone having to spot it first.
