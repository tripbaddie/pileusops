Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 26 November 2026

Pileus Ops collects only what you choose to send us, uses it to answer you, and never sells it. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers.

Who we are

Pileus Ops is an AI operations firm based in Detroit, Michigan. This policy covers pileusops.com, the forms on it and the emails we send. If you have a question about it, write to contact@pileusops.com.

What we collect

What you send us. We only receive what you type into a form and choose to send:

  • The assessment: what your company sells, the apps you run, how many people work there, the problem you picked, any cost figures you enter, and your work email if you ask for the report.
  • Book a call: your name, work email, company, the time you picked and anything you would like to cover.
  • Contact: your name, work email, company, team size and your note.
  • Email: anything you send to us or reply with.

What your browser sends. Like any website, the services that deliver this site receive your IP address and basic browser details when a page loads. GitHub hosts the site and Google delivers its fonts. We do not receive or keep these details.

Business contact details. When we reach out to a company, we may use a person's work name, job title, company and work email from public sources such as company websites and professional profiles.

What we do not collect. No cookies, no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking across other websites. We do not ask for payment details on this site.

Your assessment report

Your answers stay in your browser while you use the assessment. If you ask for the report, we email you a link that rebuilds your result from the link itself, and we receive a copy of your answers and your work email so we can follow up. Anyone with your report link can open it, so share it only with people you choose.

How we use it

  • To answer your note, arrange your call and prepare for it.
  • To send the report you asked for and follow up about it.
  • To send occasional business emails about our services. Every one is easy to opt out of.
  • To keep the site and our services working, secure and useful.
  • To meet legal obligations.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on your request (to answer you), our legitimate interest in running and growing our business (for follow-up and outreach), and legal obligation where it applies.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising across other websites. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that run our email, forms and website, such as Google and GitHub. They process it on our behalf and under their own security commitments.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect our rights and safety.
  • A buyer or successor, if Pileus Ops is ever merged or sold, under the same protections as this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep what you send for as long as we are talking with you and for up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client or the law requires longer. Then we delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Your choices and rights

  • Stop our emails. Reply "unsubscribe" or use the opt-out in any email, and we will stop.
  • See, correct or delete. Ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it.
  • US state rights. If you live in a state with a privacy law, such as California, you have the rights that law gives you, including to know, correct and delete. We will not treat you differently for using them.
  • EEA and UK rights. You can also object to or restrict how we use your information, ask to move it, and complain to your data protection authority.

Write to contact@pileusops.com with your request. We answer within 30 days, and we may ask you to confirm it is you before we act.

Security

We use reputable providers, keep access to what you send limited to the people who need it, and protect our accounts with strong sign-in controls. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, so please do not send anything sensitive, such as passwords or financial account details, through our forms.

Children

This site is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Where it is processed

We are based in the United States, and our providers process information here. If you write to us from another country, your information comes to the United States.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will say so on this page.
